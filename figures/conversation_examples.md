## Pattern Summary

| Pattern | Copy-Paste | Substitute | Complement | Behavior |
|---------|-----------|-----------|------------|----------|
| Substitute Learner | 4/5 | 4/5 | 2/5 | Pastes quiz instructions, no original thought |
| Complement Learner | 1/5 | 2/5 | 4/5 | Asks original questions, seeks understanding |
| Strategic Copier | 4/5 | 3/5 | 3/5 | Copies some questions but also asks for explanations |
